## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a finished, exceptional front cover for a serious science and computation book, flat artwork with no physical book mockup. Portrait 5:7 composition, high resolution, suitable for both print and a small mobile thumbnail.

Book title, rendered exactly and beautifully: "COMPILING THE FLY BRAIN".
Author line, small: "THE FLY-BRAIN PROJECT".
Subtitle, exactly: "What a Wiring Diagram Constrains, and What Experiments Must Decide".

Art direction: the sophistication of a museum natural-history monograph meets contemporary computational science. Deep midnight-blue, almost black matte ground with subtle paper grain. Warm ivory editorial serif typography, beautifully spaced and crisp, title large in the upper third. A single extraordinary Drosophila fruit fly dominates the lower two thirds, dorsal three-quarter view, anatomically convincing insect silhouette, delicate engraved translucent wings, amber faceted compound eyes. Its head is partly translucent, revealing a breathtaking dense structure of fine copper-gold neuronal arbors and tiny synaptic points, a small luminous compass-like neural ring within the brain. The fine network spills delicately into surrounding negative space, like a precise copperplate etching becoming a living constellation. Jewel-like amber illumination, rich restrained shadows, extraordinary microscopic detail, tangible delicacy. The insect should feel beautiful and intellectually arresting, not menacing. One luminous focal point in the head, many dim filaments, deliberate visual hierarchy, generous breathing room. Subtitle near the bottom in a small but readable ivory serif block. The title must remain immediately legible at thumbnail size.

No human brain, no robotic insect, no neon rainbow, no magenta, no generic tech circuitry, no UI panels, no additional labels, no equations, no borders, no watermark, no promotional badges. Preserve all exact text and produce a complete compelling cover, not a template.
