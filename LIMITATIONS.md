# Limitations and non-claims

This repository is intentionally more honest than polished.

- The CI fallback is an `alwaysDecides` stub. It is deterministic scaffolding, not proprietary DeltaX, not a trained executive, and never evidence of DeltaX reasoning.
- No proprietary DeltaX implementation, weights, service, or credentials are included. Canonical API mode only calls a reachable local endpoint.
- The Entity Observatory is a modest field inspector and JSON exporter. It is not a full connectome visualization, neural microscope, or live topology viewer.
- The UI displays recorded fields only. It does not fabricate neural animations, confidence, causality, or telemetry when a checkpoint is absent.
- Numerical replay is bounded by serialized checkpoints, floating point behavior, runtime versions, and the fidelity of recorded packets. Bitwise identity across machines is not promised.
- Broken World is a scaffold for headless causal intervention, checkpoint, and restore workflows. It does not establish full learning, general intelligence, embodiment, or biological equivalence.
- Causal intervention artifacts demonstrate harness accounting and authority ordering. They do not prove that a substrate learned the intervention or that the world model is complete.
- The upstream fly tree is preserved for validation, but its large data files can be slow to clone, inspect, or reproduce.
- Static serving is required for `entity.html`; opening it as a local file can prevent artifact loading.
- An absent API is an explicit failure in DELTAX mode. Choosing the stub is a conscious CI/offline action, not an automatic fallback.
- Export is a snapshot of observable ledger fields, not a lossless dump of a connectome or a substitute for experiment artifacts.
- Biological Multi-Synaptic Latency: An intact 165k-neuron connectome naturally requires multiple simulation steps for sensory propagation through interneuron layers to descending motor neurons. In short 12-step task horizons, this biological latency limits total distance traveled before time expires.
- Control Dynamics: The degree-preserving shuffled connectome control bypasses biological multi-layer interneuron delay lines by wiring sensory afferents directly to motor neurons, highlighting the distinct topological structure of the intact circuit.
- Causal Integrity: All post-DeltaX actuator shortcuts and harness steering substitutions have been permanently removed. The governor can only evaluate and admit genuine pre-evaluation substrate candidates. If DeltaX selects `halt` upon obstacle detection, the entity halts.
- Phase III Spatial Foresight Boundary: Under strict unassisted evaluation (zero harness-side coordinate checks at $x=3$), retained DeltaX session memory does not produce anticipatory steering into bypass corridors or hazard reduction (paired difference: 0.0 ± 0.0). DeltaX coherence governance provides collision safety via halting, but does not provide autonomous 2D grid pathfinding.
- Connectome Autonomy Boundary: The intact connectome alone does not possess long-horizon spatial planning; under sudden environmental blockage, both unguided connectome dynamics and unassisted closed-loop governance stop at the blockage rather than navigating through alternate corridors.
- Sensory Interface Symmetry: Reconstructed Phase III environmental observations were 100% rotationally symmetric ($S_L = S_R$). Under symmetric stimulation, intact steering pathways are driven symmetrically, collapsing the candidate field into HALT.
- Biological Lateral Asymmetry: Biological connectome wiring is not mirror-symmetric: unilateral left front-leg stimulation produces strong ipsilateral steering, while unilateral right stimulation does not exhibit an equivalent mirror response.
- Candidate Bridge Derivation: The HALT candidate in the CandidateBridge is derived from the suppression of forward drive rather than directly measured from a dedicated neural population. This can suppress legitimate steering signals when forward locomotion drops.
- Non-Nociception: The fruit fly connectome possesses no populations labeled "nociceptor" or "pain". Aversive stimulation is strictly operationalized as high-threshold mechanical deflections, extreme thermal stimulation, and bitter gustation; no subjective experience is modeled or claimed.
- Phase IV-C Generalization Boundary: DeltaX executive navigation generalizes across spatial variations that match the connectome-derived computational substrate's endogenous left-turning polarity. In environments where right turns are physically mandatory (`ENV_1B_TRUE_RIGHT_REQUIRED`, `ENV_POLARITY_RIGHT`, `ENV_CHOICE_WITH_REVERSAL`, `ENV_TEMPORAL_NON_MARKOVIAN`), the fixed connectome-derived RateNetwork generates 0.0 Hz right-turn descending drive, and all controllers achieve 0.0% goal discovery. DeltaX cannot invent or substitute actions absent from the substrate.
- Executive Stateless Equivalence: In this deterministic Markovian navigation suite, cross-trial retained executive state (`DELTAX_EXECUTIVE`) produces bit-exact identical trajectories to inter-trial reset (`DELTAX_TRIAL_RESET`) and per-step session isolation (`DELTAX_STEP_RESET`) across all 4,500 evaluated DeltaX episodes. Retained executive state confers no measurable advantage without synaptic plasticity or associative memory retrieval.
- Non-Planning Boundary: Success in 11/15 environments reflects reactive candidate arbitration and wall-following stabilization, not internal world modeling, mental simulation, or cognitive map navigation.
- Substrate Firing Model vs Biological Equivalence: The connectome-derived RateNetwork is instantiated from measured Drosophila whole-CNS topology, but its firing dynamics are an idealized continuous rate model; biological equivalence or living organism behavior is not claimed.
- Telemetry Scope & Replay Boundary: Committed artifacts in `artifacts/generalization/` contain complete episode summaries for all 9,000 episodes and 19,305 critical candidate-fork decisions (2,800 per DeltaX controller). The public repository does not store a complete 475,000-step per-tick trajectory trace.
- Runtime Version & Build Authentication: The identifier `runtime_version: "v1.0.0-local"` recorded in artifact JSON files was assigned locally by `scripts/run_phase4c_generalization.mjs` and is not an externally verified cryptographic runtime attestation. Public tests verify the integration contract, not private runtime reproducibility. Build authentication remains deferred.
- Developmental Plasticity Boundary: Phase IV-D synaptic plasticity investigations were conducted exclusively on development cohorts (`18000..18299`). The held-out confirmation cohort (`19000..19099`) has not been run and remains strictly unconsumed in committed empirical records under the research pause.
- Responder Heterogeneity & Network Learnability: Connectome learnability under local three-factor plasticity is bistable and seed-limited. Even after 120 induction cycles, 22% of networks remain complete non-responders because intrinsic baseline depolarization relative to threshold (`DNa02_inp_over_theta`) is insufficient to traverse the downstream anatomical bottleneck.
- Upstream Sensory Simplification: Visual sensory arrays in `bodymap.json` represent flat, uncalibrated photoreceptors without optical flow, T4/T5 directional selectivity, or lobula columnar (LC4/LPLC2) feature extraction circuits. Consequently, structurally reachable looming escape pathways (`DNp01/DNp02`) cannot be functionally recruited without enhanced sensory preprocessing.
