## 1. Executive Summary

This audit evaluates the mapping between descending neuron (DN) behavioral candidates emitted by the connectome candidate bridge and the physical actuator vocabulary supported by the `RoverBody` embodiment.

Prior to Phase IV-C, the `RoverBody` possessed only four actions: `forward`, `left`, `right`, and `stop`. As a result, critical motor programs generated by biological descending populations—notably backward locomotion driven by Moonwalker Descending Neurons (`MDN`) and ballistic escape driven by Giant Fibre (`DNp01`)—were silently collapsed into `stop`.

In this audit, we complete the actuator vocabulary by implementing true `backward` locomotion, classify each biological candidate's embodiment status, and mandate explicit provenance handling so that unembodied neural programs cannot be masqueraded as physical actions.

## 3. Detailed Actuator Specifications

### 3.1 `backward` Locomotion (Newly Embodied)
- **Actuator Name:** `backward`
- **Heading Behavior:** Strictly invariant. The heading angle is completely unchanged (`nextHeading = currentHeading`).
- **Kinematic Delta:** Grid displacement occurs opposite the current heading vector:
  $$\Delta x = -\Delta x_{\text{heading}}, \quad \Delta y = -\Delta y_{\text{heading}}$$
- **Collision Checking:** `canMove(target)` evaluated at the reverse cell. If occupied by wall/door, returns `status: "BLOCKED"`, increments collision telemetry, and position remains unchanged.
- **Energy Cost:** Exact cost of 1.0 unit (matched to forward translation).
- **Telemetry Signature:** `status: "MOVED_BACKWARD"`, recording new coordinates $(x, y)$ and updated energy.

### 3.2 Handling of `giant_fiber_escape`
In real fruit flies, Giant Fibre (`DNp01`) activation triggers tergotrochanteral muscle (`TTM`) contraction, launching a ballistic jump over looming obstacles. In a 2D grid world:
- Mapping `escape` $\to$ `stop` was a silent failure of embodiment fidelity (falsely representing a panic jump as braking).
- Inventing an artificial teleportation or obstacle-jumping mechanic would introduce a task-specific cheat.
- **Honest Governance Solution:** `giant_fiber_escape` is explicitly typed as `UNEMBODIED`. It remains visible in the candidate field for neuroscience observability, but its `is_executable` flag is `false` and governance filters it as actuator-unavailable.

## 4. Verification Invariants

1. **Vocabulary Invariant:** Any action submitted to `RoverBody.apply()` must belong to `['forward', 'backward', 'left', 'right', 'stop']`.
2. **Provenance Typing Invariant:** Every executed action must correspond to a candidate generated prior to evaluation.
3. **Actuator Availability Invariant:** Candidates marked `is_executable: false` or `embodiment_status: "UNEMBODIED"` must never be dispatched to the actuator.
