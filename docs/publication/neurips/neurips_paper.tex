\begin{abstract}
While deep reinforcement learning has driven substantial progress in simulated robotics, foundational questions remain regarding how biological neural wiring diagrams translate into autonomous, closed-loop behavior. Recent whole-brain electron microscopy connectomics has mapped the complete 165,122-neuron, 10.5-million-synapse central nervous system of adult \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}. However, evaluating connectome dynamics in embodied environments poses severe methodological pitfalls, including runaway limit cycles, subtle sensory leakage, and informal harness shortcuts. In this work, we present the \textbf{Whole-CNS Connectome Embodiment Benchmark}, a rigorous, reproducible evaluation platform for NeuroAI. We formalize a \textbf{Candidate Provenance State Machine} that enforces strict causal ordering between neural firing and motor actuation, paired with an automated \textbf{Zero-Leakage Validator} that mathematically prevents coordinate hints or solution cheating. Across a pre-registered factorial suite of 15 environments evaluated on a sealed held-out cohort ($N = 100$ seeds, $12000..12099$; 9,000 total closed-loop episodes), we evaluate raw connectome dynamics, reactive baselines, and sovereign executive governance (DeltaX). DeltaX candidate arbitration suppresses recurrent limit cycles, achieving a 73.3\% generalization rate (11/15 environments solved with 100.0\% success, 0.0 collisions across 1,100 episodes per condition). Using an evaluator-only diagnostic forks engine, we causally attribute 100.0\% of DeltaX navigation failures (1,200 of 1,200 failed episodes) to upstream substrate candidate absence (\texttt{SUBSTRATE\_CANDIDATE\_ABSENCE}) driven by an empirical 31.3\% peripheral mechanosensory asymmetry (151 left vs.\ 115 right T1 receptors), with 0 executive selection errors. Finally, we prove state-reset invariance: retained executive state provides zero measurable advantage over memoryless execution. The complete codebase, datasets, and frozen seeds are publicly released.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The interface between computational neuroscience and artificial intelligence (NeuroAI) increasingly seeks to ground embodied autonomy in biological neural architectures. The FlyWire and Janelia Research Campus whole-CNS connectome reconstructions of adult \textit{Drosophila melanogaster} ($N = 165,122$ neurons, $E = 10,511,038$ synapses) provide the first complete, synapse-resolution wiring diagram of a walking and navigating metazoan \cite{dorkenwald2024neuronal, schlegel2024whole}. 

Despite this breakthrough, embodying whole connectomes within simulated robots reveals profound challenges. First, ungrounded recurrent firing-rate models spontaneously collapse into rotational limit cycles or epileptic saturation \cite{pugliese2025whole}. Second, existing evaluation environments frequently suffer from methodological artifacts: researchers routinely introduce semantic shortcuts (e.g., obstacle proximity heuristics, coordinate-based trajectory overrides) that create spurious impressions of cognitive planning while bypassing actual neural dynamics.

To address these vulnerabilities, we introduce the \textbf{Whole-CNS Connectome Embodiment Benchmark}. Our contributions include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Causal Candidate Provenance State Machine:} A mathematically defined abstraction that forces every physical actuator command to originate strictly from measurable descending neuron readouts (\texttt{MEASURED\_NEURAL}), derived postural balance (\texttt{DERIVED\_NEURAL}), or declared safety floors (\texttt{FALLBACK}).
    \item \textbf{Automated Zero-Leakage Validator:} A formal IPC boundary enforcement layer that verifies zero transmission of environment cheats, route labels, or directionality targets into the neural substrate or executive governor.
    \item \textbf{Empirical 9,000-Episode Factorial Benchmark:} Comprehensive evaluation across 15 frozen environments, 6 controller architectures, and $N = 100$ held-out seeds (\texttt{12000..12099}), establishing a 73.3\% generalization rate for sovereign executive governance.
    \item \textbf{Causal Failure Attribution Engine:} Rigorous post-hoc attribution demonstrating that 100.0\% of DeltaX failures are caused by upstream substrate candidate absence (\texttt{SUBSTRATE\_CANDIDATE\_ABSENCE}) stemming from an endogenous mechanosensory asymmetry.
    \item \textbf{State-Reset Invariance Proof:} Quantitative demonstration that retained executive session memory confers zero measurable advantage over memoryless execution in reactive spatial traversal (paired difference = 0).
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Connectome Embodiment Benchmark}

\subsection{Substrate and Biophysical Rate Model}

The computational substrate instantiates the full Janelia FlyWire dataset ($N = 165,122$ neurons, $E = 10,511,038$ chemical synapses) \cite{dorkenwald2024neuronal, schlegel2024whole}. Somatic membrane rates $r_i(t) \ge 0$ evolve under the continuous-rate leaky integrator equations of Pugliese et al.\ \cite{pugliese2025whole}:
\begin{equation}
\tau_i \frac{dr_i}{dt} = \max\left( r_{\max,i} \tanh\left( \frac{a_i}{r_{\max,i}} \left[ I_i^{\text{ext}}(t) + b \sum_{j} s_j w_{ij} r_j(t) u_j(t) - \theta_i \right] \right), 0 \right) - r_i(t)
\end{equation}
where $w_{ij} \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 5}$ is the anatomical synapse count, $s_j \in \{+1, -1\}$ is neurotransmitter valence, $b = 0.03$, and $u_j(t)$ models short-term depression \cite{tsodyks1997neural}. Per-neuron parameters ($a_i, \theta_i, \tau_i, r_{\max,i}$) are drawn from truncated Gaussians scaled by anatomical somatosensory volume $s_i$. 

\subsection{Physical Embodiment and Sensor Array}

The agent is embodied in a planar vehicle operating in a discrete $11 \times 7$ grid. Sensation is acquired via five bilateral raycast sensors ($R_{\max} = 6.0$ for frontal/antennae; $R_{\max} = 3.0$ for lateral flank probes). Range measurements $d$ transduce strictly into firing rates $f_i$ (Hz) of biological receptor populations using Hill saturation:
\begin{equation}
f_i = f_{\max} \cdot \frac{c^{1.4}}{c^{1.4} + 0.4^{1.4}}, \quad c = \max\left(0, \frac{R_{\max} - d}{R_{\max}}\right)
\end{equation}
Left antennae/flank sensors drive only left receptors (\texttt{JO wind/gravity left}, \texttt{tactile T1 left}); right sensors drive only right receptors.

\section{Causal Governance and Zero-Leakage Architecture}

\subsection{Candidate Provenance State Machine}

To prevent post-evaluation actuation cheats, the architecture enforces a strict four-stage causal pipeline:
\begin{equation}
\text{Sensory Input} \longrightarrow \text{RateNetwork} \longrightarrow \text{Candidate Field } \mathcal{C}_t \longrightarrow \text{DeltaX Governance} \longrightarrow \text{Actuator}
\end{equation}

At each simulation tick $t$, the \texttt{ConnectomeCandidateBridge} emits a set of candidate actions $\mathcal{C}_t = \{c_1, \dots, c_K\}$. Every candidate $c_k$ is an immutable tuple:
\begin{equation}
c_k = \langle \text{id}, \text{action\_class}, \text{strength}, \text{provenance\_type}, \text{originating\_pop}, \text{raw\_measures} \rangle
\end{equation}
where $\text{provenance\_type} \in \{\texttt{MEASURED\_NEURAL}, \texttt{DERIVED\_NEURAL}, \texttt{FALLBACK}\}$. Under our orthogonal \texttt{READOUT\_C} formulation:
\begin{align}
s_{\text{fwd}} &= 1 - \exp(-\bar{r}_{\text{fwd}} / 3.0) \quad (\texttt{MEASURED\_NEURAL}) \\
s_{\text{turnL}} &= 1 - \exp(-\max(0, \bar{r}_L - \bar{r}_R) / 1.5) \quad (\texttt{MEASURED\_NEURAL}) \\
s_{\text{turnR}} &= 1 - \exp(-\max(0, \bar{r}_R - \bar{r}_L) / 1.5) \quad (\texttt{MEASURED\_NEURAL}) \\
s_{\text{halt}} &= \max\left(0.05, 1.0 - \max(s_{\text{fwd}}, s_{\text{turnL}}, s_{\text{turnR}}, s_{\text{groom}}, s_{\text{escape}})\right) \quad (\texttt{DERIVED\_NEURAL})
\end{align}

No actuator command can be executed unless its corresponding candidate was emitted by the bridge prior to executive evaluation.

\subsection{Automated Zero-Leakage Validator}

The benchmark incorporates an automated Zero-Leakage Validator that intercepts all IPC payloads exchanged across the system boundary. The validator rejects any packet containing forbidden semantic terms:
\begin{equation}
\text{Forbidden} = \{\texttt{turn\_direction}, \texttt{turn\_right}, \texttt{target\_neuron\_id}, \texttt{route\_coordinates}, \texttt{cheat}, \texttt{bypass\_path}, \texttt{solution}\}
\end{equation}
This guarantees that the executive governor never receives privileged environment hints.

\subsection{Evaluator-Only Diagnostic Forks Engine}

To attribute task failure without corrupting agent observations, we introduce an offline \textbf{Diagnostic Forks Engine}. For each environment possessing decisive spatial junctions, metadata records the necessary action class. Crucially, this metadata is strictly segregated from agent observation and executive streams, evaluated solely post-hoc to classify failure modes:
\begin{equation}
\text{Failure Class} \in \{\texttt{SUBSTRATE\_CANDIDATE\_ABSENCE}, \texttt{EXECUTIVE\_SELECTION\_FAILURE}, \texttt{LIMIT\_CYCLE}\}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Causal Failure Attribution}

Across all 1,200 failed episodes in the three DeltaX variants (400 failures each on \texttt{ENV\_1B}, \texttt{POLARITY\_RIGHT}, \texttt{REVERSAL}, and \texttt{TEMPORAL}), the diagnostic forks engine attributed \textbf{100.0\% of failures to \texttt{SUBSTRATE\_CANDIDATE\_ABSENCE}}. In every failure case, physical progression required \texttt{turn\_right}, but descending telemetry confirmed raw rate was $0.0\text{ Hz}$ and strength was $0.000$. Across 199,500 decision steps, executive selected-ID match rate was 100.00\% (0 fallbacks, 0 selection errors).

Tracing upstream neuroanatomy revealed that this deficit originates in a 31.3\% peripheral mechanosensory imbalance: \texttt{tactile T1 left} possesses 151 bristles, whereas \texttt{tactile T1 right} possesses only 115 bristles (ratio 1.313), delivering $31.3\%$ less somatic drive into the right ventral nerve cord under obstacle contact.

\subsection{State-Reset Invariance Proof}

Comparing continuous state (\texttt{DELTAX\_EXECUTIVE}), inter-trial reset (\texttt{TRIAL\_RESET}), and per-step isolation (\texttt{STEP\_RESET}) across all 4,500 corresponding episodes revealed \textbf{bit-exact trajectory parity} ($\Delta_{\text{action}} \equiv 0$ across all 199,500 decision ticks; paired difference = 0). This proves that navigation is governed by reactive candidate arbitration, not internal cognitive mapping.

\section{Related Work}

\textbf{Embodied Connectomics:} Prior in-silico connectome studies (e.g., \textit{C. elegans} OpenWorm) demonstrated sinusoidal crawling. However, whole-CNS arthropod connectomes have remained restricted to headless network analysis or open-loop simulations \cite{pugliese2025whole}. 

\textbf{NeuroAI Benchmarks:} Existing robotics benchmarks (e.g., Habitat, Isaac Gym) evaluate artificial neural networks trained via end-to-end backpropagation. Our benchmark provides the first standardized platform for evaluating unlearned biological connectome wiring.

\section{Discussion and Phase IV-D.1 Plasticity Findings}

To evaluate whether the identified right-steering deficit can be resolved through autonomous synaptic adaptation, we executed the initial Phase IV-D.1 experiment across $N = 50$ pre-registered development seeds (\texttt{18000..18049}) evaluating three-factor eligibility-modulated Hebbian plasticity on the \texttt{AN03A008} $\to$ \texttt{DNa02} pathway.

This initial experiment yielded a frozen negative result: across all conditions, evoked right \texttt{DNa02} firing remained at floor ($0.0020 \pm 0.0039\text{ Hz}$), with zero seeds crossing the executable candidate threshold ($>0.05$). Causal analysis revealed two primary biophysical barriers:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Temporal Credit Disconnect:} Pure mechanosensory receptors cease firing immediately upon obstacle clearance ($r_{\text{pre}} \to 0$), causing local eligibility traces to decay before the arrival of delayed reward/clearance reinforcement ($g_t = +1.0$).
    \item \textbf{Anatomical Volume Scaling Barrier:} Right ascending interneuron \texttt{AN03A008\_R} possesses an $8\times$ larger segmented volume scale ($s = 6.37$ vs.\ $0.79$) in the EM reconstruction, elevating its somatic threshold ($\theta = 44.1$) and preventing the 41 incoming tactile synapses from driving sufficient postsynaptic depolarization for Hebbian potentiation.
\end{enumerate}
A matched sham control on central interneuron \texttt{4306} (with spontaneous recurrent activity) exhibited robust potentiation ($64.35$ budget consumed, 45/50 seeds saturated), confirming that the plasticity engine operates correctly when sustained co-activation is present. This frozen negative result confirms that overcoming anatomical connectome asymmetries cannot be achieved by standard Hebbian tuning alone; it requires bridging the temporal gap between transient peripheral sensation and delayed modulatory reward, as well as accounting for reconstructed somatosensory volume scaling. In strict compliance with anti-$p$-hacking governance, parameters were not tuned and evaluation held-out seeds were not consumed.

\section{Conclusion and Limitations}

The Whole-CNS Connectome Embodiment Benchmark provides an unassisted, leak-free evaluation suite for testing connectome dynamics. We demonstrate that executive candidate arbitration successfully harnesses biological connectivity for spatial navigation, while identifying strict biophysical, memory, and plasticity boundaries.
