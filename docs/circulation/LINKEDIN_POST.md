# LinkedIn post draft

**Owner publishes; this file is not a record of publication.**

I have paused the DeltaX Connectome Entity research project and published the evidence boundary as it stands.

The project tests DeltaX as an external, candidate-constrained governance layer over an idealized connectome-derived rate model. The central result is a constraint, not a capability claim: the governor never invented an action the substrate did not propose. In the frozen Phase IV-C benchmark, 199,500 of 199,500 selections matched a substrate candidate, with zero fallbacks and zero selection errors.

Across 100 held-out seeds and 15 environments, 11 were solved at 100% seed parity. The four failures are just as important. Each environment required a right turn, and the substrate supplied no right-turn candidate. Every controller failed at the same boundary.

I also kept the nulls after removing actuator shortcuts that had made earlier results look stronger. Those early 100% lifts remain archived as `superseded_pre_integrity_pass`; they are not presented as current evidence.

The public repository includes the adapter, contracts, tests, claims register, limitations, and frozen traces. It does not reproduce the private DeltaX runtime, and the model is not a living organism. Plasticity results remain development-only, with held-out seeds 19000..19099 sealed.
