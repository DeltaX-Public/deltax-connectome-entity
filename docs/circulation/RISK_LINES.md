# Public-circulation risk lines

Use these sentences with excerpts, screenshots, or posts when the surrounding context would otherwise disappear.

## Hype-collapse trap

**Risk:** The connectome scale or “executive control plane” language is quoted as a claim of organism-level intelligence, planning, or general agency.

**Pre-empting sentence:** This is an idealized connectome-derived rate model, not a living organism; the result is candidate-constrained governance, not a claim of AGI, consciousness, biological equivalence, or long-horizon planning.

## Reproducibility trap

**Risk:** A reader runs public tests and concludes that the clone reproduced or authenticated the private DeltaX runtime.

**Pre-empting sentence:** Public tests verify the adapter, candidate constraint, committed traces, pause state, and evidence hashes; they do not reproduce the private DeltaX runtime, and `v1.0.0-local` is not cryptographic attestation.

## Integrity-walkback trap

**Risk:** Archived early 100% lifts are repeated without the methodological correction that invalidated them.

**Pre-empting sentence:** Early 100% lifts used actuator shortcuts and are archived as `superseded_pre_integrity_pass`; current claims use the post-integrity candidate-constrained architecture and retain its null results.
