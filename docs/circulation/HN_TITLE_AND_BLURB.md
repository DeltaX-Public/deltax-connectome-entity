## Title

Show HN: A frozen evaluation of candidate-constrained AI governance

## Four-sentence first comment

I built this repository to test DeltaX as an external governor constrained to actions proposed by an idealized connectome-derived rate model. In the frozen Phase IV-C benchmark, all 199,500 selections matched substrate candidate IDs, with zero fallbacks and zero selection errors. Across 100 held-out seeds and 15 environments, 11 were solved at 100% seed parity, while four right-turn-required environments failed because the substrate produced no right-turn candidate. The project is now paused, the held-out plasticity seeds remain sealed, the private DeltaX runtime is not reproduced here, and DeltaX-owned material is under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 rather than an unqualified open-source license.
